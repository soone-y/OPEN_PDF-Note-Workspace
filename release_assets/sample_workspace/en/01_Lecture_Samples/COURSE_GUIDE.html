Course-style sample folders. - `Session_01_Basics`: the first-launch pair of a PDF guide and standard note for viewing, annotation, editing, and saving. - `Session_02_Note_Formats`: material for directly opening Markdown, TeX source, plain text, CSV, and standard notes by format. Its standard note and PDF link practice also demonstrate a round-trip link between a note and a PDF. - `Session_03_Early_Design`: an English reference PDF for a historical early-design exercise. It is not the current specification. Includes a `.clro` note. - `Session_04_Office_Conversion`: one PowerPoint-derived PDF with a native chart, converted by the local LibreOffice runtime, plus a `.clro` note. The Full edition contains all four sessions. Lite omits Session 04 and its conversion-result PDF, but includes Session 02.
